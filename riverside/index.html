Two common situations call for a quick step from you before our technician gets there. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: turn the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so warm indoor air thaws the coil, because running it frozen can flood the drain pan and damage the compressor. The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or gives off a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and call us. Those few minutes can prevent a far bigger repair.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Riverside

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and far less stressful than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
